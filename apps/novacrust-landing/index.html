Questions

Which networks do you support?

Placeholder list: USDC and USDT on Tron, Ethereum, Solana and Base. Sending on an unsupported network can mean lost funds, so check the network before you send.

What if my transfer is delayed?

Most delays come from network congestion or a bank-side hold. You can track status in the app, and our support team can look into any transfer that's slower than expected.

Is it safe?

There's no P2P counterparty, so there's no stranger who can fail to pay you. You review the full cost before confirming, and we never ask for your wallet's private keys or seed phrase.
